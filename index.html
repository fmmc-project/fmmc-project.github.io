Abstract

Material classification is important for visual understanding, graphics, and embodied AI, but large-scale annotated material datasets remain limited. FMMC leverages foundation models to improve material classification through synthetic data generation, automatic labeling, and vision-language prior guided fine-tuning.

Method

Our framework consists of three key components:

  • Material-centric image generation using foundation models.
  • Automatic label assignment by combining object semantics and material attributes.
  • Joint fine-tuning with vision-language priors for accurate material recognition.
